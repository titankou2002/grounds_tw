01

Why Now

Taiwan is a first-to-file jurisdiction — first to file wins.

Situation

grounds is already sold in Taiwan through select shops (Plain-me, NE.SENSE, UJNG), with media coverage (TVBS 07/2026) and a pop-up event.

But the brand has zero trademark protection in Taiwan — anyone could register it at any time.

The priority position takes effect from the filing date — no need to wait for approval. The sooner we file, the better.

04

Goods & Classes

File three classes at once: footwear + bags + retail services.

Class 25 — Footwear/Apparel

  • Footwear mainly (sneakers, platform, MOOPIE Mary Janes, knit shoes)
  • Clothing, headwear

Class 18 — Bags/Leather

  • Handbags, shoulder bags, travel bags, wallets, leather goods

Class 35 — Retail/E-commerce ★

Retail/wholesale of footwear, clothing, bags; and online (e-commerce) purchase services.

This protects the service of "selling grounds", not just the goods.

Note: beyond 5 specified items, official fee adds NT$500 per item — ask the agent to keep it lean.

Expedition note

Expedited means faster first examination notice — not guaranteed registration. Requires "in use / imminent use + urgency".

Our plan: start standard (NT$30,000 secures the filing date), prepare use evidence in parallel, evaluate expedition only if urgency arises.

07

Risk

To Guard Against

  • Squatting by third parties (brand is exposed & already sold)
  • Similar marks (GROUND FIVE, LITTLEGROUND)
  • Class 35 gap (both Taiwan & Japan)
  • Foreign entities must use a Taiwan agent

After Registration

  • 10-year term, renewable
  • "Unused" or "no use for 3+ years" can lead to cancellation
  • Keep use evidence (mark, date, user)
  • Territorial — cross-border needs per-country filing
