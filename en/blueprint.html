Current Progress (as of Oct 2026)

We are proceeding with a Taiwan subsidiary. We received two quotations dated 2026/9/30 from D&C CPAs and Associates, and are viewing several store locations.

StructureProceeding with a Taiwan subsidiary (local entity)
CPA quote 1: Subsidiary (company limited by shares / limited company) setupNT$75,000 (covers name/address pre-check, FIA investment approval, capital verification, registration, tax registration, Industrial & Commercial IC card, bank account support)
CPA quote 2 (reference): Taiwan branch setupNT$55,000 (not chosen)
Government fees (at cost)Name pre-check NT$165 / IC card NT$420 / registration = 1/4000 of capital (minimum NT$1,000)
Other costs (at cost)Seal making, postage, translation of Japanese documents; special licenses not included
Payment termsAfter signing and returning the quote by PDF, a 50% deposit is invoiced
Decisions needed from JapanCapital amount, representative, shareholder name (registration fee depends on capital)
StoreSeveral candidate locations being viewed (details at Friday's meeting)

Current Initial Direction

We recommend a Taiwan subsidiary, 100% owned by Japan.

Reason: greater flexibility for brand control,

governance, store expansion, and future growth.
